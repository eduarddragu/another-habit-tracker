# CLAUDE.md

Personal Android habit tracker (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3). The owner's private notes (background, decisions, device quirks, the weekly review) live outside the repo in `~/Repositories/.claude/context/another-habit-tracker.md`: read them first when they exist.

## Rules

- **This repository is public.** Never commit signing material, passwords, personal data, exports, logs of sessions or review notes. Anything personal goes in the private notes above, never here.
- Never change `applicationId` (`dev.eduarddragu.anotherhabittracker`) or the signing setup: either one breaks in-place updates and loses data on the phone.
- Don't commit or push unless explicitly asked.
- Code, comments, docs and UI strings in English. Plain prose, no em dashes.
- Use the `android` CLI (with `--no-metrics`) and `android docs search` for current API guidance instead of relying on memory. Verify on the device with `android screen capture` / `android layout` when a change is visible.

## Layout

- `domain/`: pure Kotlin, unit tested: streaks, freezes, reminder timing, tone and copy, the home's greeting (rotating per time of day, stable within a day) and line of the day, `Practices` (meditation's daily practice, a fixed shuffled cycle of original three-step practices), heatmap cells, stats, curriculum and topic picker, and `HabitSummaries.build` (everything a screen, widget or reminder needs about a habit on a day). `PhoneClock` follows the phone's current timezone.
- `data/`: Room entities (`Habit`, `Entry`), DAOs, `HabitRepository` (the current day comes from a minute poll plus `refreshDay()`, which the midnight and clock receivers call, since the poll stalls in deep sleep). "Keep going" (a deep dive): `Continuation.candidate` offers the last session's topic (yesterday or the day before, nothing logged today) on that session's row in Recent; `FocusStore` holds the choice for today and the repository swaps it in as the pick (`PickKind.CONTINUE`), so screens, widget and reminders agree; "Back to today's topic" on the topic card undoes it. `PickHistory` keeps the study topic each day last showed (14 days, SharedPreferences); the log form's "Yesterday" takes its topic from there, falling back to `TopicPicker.pickOn` (the pick as it was on that day). Statuses are computed once per change on `Dispatchers.Default` and shared (`shareIn`) by every screen, the widget and `HabitApp`. Schemas are exported to `app/schemas/` and committed.
- `reminders/`: notification actions go through `NotificationActionReceiver` ("Done" for habits without a score logs with the last session's minutes; "On it" sets `QuietStore` for two hours, and `ReminderPlan.quieted` skips every tone but the last call); at most three actions show, so study gets Log and On it, simple habits Done, their app and On it. `ReminderScheduler` (one exact alarm per habit and slot, at most 24 slots, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`), `ReminderReceiver` (reschedules its slot, then notifies only if the day is still open), `RescheduleReceiver` (boot, update, time and timezone changes; only after a reboot it posts the latest missed slot once: the phone corrects its clock every day or two, and posting then brought back reminders already swiped away), `MidnightReceiver` (exact alarm at 00:00:30: clears yesterday's reminders, moves the day on, renews every reminder chain, rolls widgets over, asks for a backup), `Notifications` (channels `reminders_v4` and `last_call_v4`, with synthesized chimes addressed by name, `raw/<name>`, kept from the shrinker by `res/raw/keep.xml`, and vibration; channel sound/vibration can't be changed after creation, so behaviour changes need new ids). Receiver work runs in `HabitApp.appScope`, whose exception handler logs instead of crashing.
- Log form: `LogEntry(habitId, entryId)`; with an `entryId` it edits or deletes that session ("Edit session", Save changes, Delete session with a confirmation). New logs start on yesterday before 04:00 when yesterday is empty (`LogDefaults`); the score line comes from `TopicPicker.scoreMeaning`. Recent lists sessions and freezes (a freeze is a hollow dot and can only be removed); the streak line says whether this week's freeze is used, and a missed yesterday can be frozen from the habit page (`Freezes.canSaveYesterday`). "I know this" and "Mark the whole area as known" come with an Undo snackbar.
- `ui/`: Compose screens (home, habit detail with today's topic, log, curriculum, habit settings) on Navigation 3. Screens are edge to edge: they fill the window and use `screenPadding()` as scroll content padding. Per-habit ViewModels extend `HabitViewModel` (live status, `once {}` guard against double taps). Habit icons are line vectors in `res/drawable/ic_habit_*.xml` (24dp, 1.75 stroke), chosen per habit (`Habit.icon`, a `HabitIcon` name; null means the kind's default). Home is three zones down the screen: date, greeting (the name in the accent italic, revealed by a soft left-to-right edge while it sharpens from a blur) and line; the `Planet` mark (butter disc, tilted ring, from eduarddragu.dev; endless idle loop while visible: the ring swings, opens and closes, the disc breathes) in the middle; one card per habit at the bottom. The full entrance plays at app start; coming back from outside the app replays the planet and the cards (reset on ON_STOP so they never blink off on screen), coming back from a habit's page brings the cards up again, lighter, with the slide. The streak and `WeekStrip` (this week, Monday to Sunday) are on every home card, the detail page and the widget; the heatmap only appears after eight weeks of history (`Heatmap.worthShowing`) and starts at the first log. Logging plays a commit on the screen underneath: the log form posts a `Commit` after the write, and `rememberCommit` fills the day, rolls the streak (`RollingNumber`) and swaps the labels once the sheet is gone (timings in `Motion.Commit`), with the Confirm haptic; a same-day log shows no snackbar. Minutes are presets only (study 30/45/60, other habits 5/10/15). Shared building blocks in `ui/components/Common.kt`: `SectionLabel` (every section heading: mono capitals in the accent), `DayCard` (today's topic or practice: plain while open, warm once done, explicit onSurface content color), `NumberedSteps`, `LogButton` ("Log" / "Log again"), `cardOutline()` (outlined buttons on tonal cards), `Chevron`, `TextAction` (accent text with no button box, for every text action at a row's edge or on its own line: a TextButton centres short labels in a 58dp minimum width, so they never sit flush, and its 48dp target makes lines taller). Rules: every card uses `shapes.large` (24dp), controls `shapes.medium` (12dp); sections 24dp apart, 8dp inside, 12dp inside cards; card labels are muted with only the state in the accent; text buttons trailing a row are flush right (zero horizontal padding), leading ones flush left; numbers use `NumeralsDisplay` / `NumeralsLarge` / `Numerals` / `NumeralsSmall`; swaps use `fadeThrough()`. Mixed faces or sizes on one line align by baseline (`alignByBaseline`, `alignBy(LastBaseline)`), never by box; big numbers use `opticalStart` so the first digit's empty side (DM Sans' "1" especially) doesn't look like an indent; the Recent timeline dot is placed from the date's measured baseline minus half the mono cap height. `theme/` holds the palette (the accent is a touch deeper in light, #B04619, and lighter in dark, #E8743F, than the site's, so small accent text reaches 4.5:1 on the tonal cards), the bundled fonts (Cormorant Garamond for titles, always with lining figures (`lnum`), since its default old-style digits read as letters; DM Sans for text and for values via `Numerals` (DM Sans has no tabular figures), Geist Mono for small labels; all four font files are subset to Latin, punctuation and arrows with fonttools (`pyftsubset --unicodes=U+0000-00FF,U+0131,U+0152-0153,U+02BB-02BC,U+02C6,U+02DA,U+02DC,U+2000-206F,U+2074,U+20AC,U+2122,U+2190-2193,U+2212,U+2215,U+FEFF,U+FFFD --layout-features='*' --no-hinting`), so a character outside that set in the curriculum or copy needs a new subset; OFL licenses in `assets/licenses/`) and `Motion` (easing and durations from the site: slide transitions, never fade two screens at once; the push is 520 ms so it matches the slow home, and a pushed page's content arrives in steps via `rememberArrival`, once per page).
- `backup/`: `BackupStore` and `BackupWorker`. One versioned JSON document (`domain/Backup.kt`, DTOs separate from Room, strict validation, a golden v1 file in the tests). Manual export and import through SAF; import replaces everything in one transaction after saving the current data to `before-import-<time>.json`. A nightly file chosen once (persisted grant, e.g. on Drive) is rewritten by a WorkManager job two minutes after every change and after midnight, then read back to verify; a local copy goes to the app's external files folder for `adb pull`. Status shows at the bottom of Home, as a warning when it failed or is 48 h old. Bump `Backups.VERSION` for any field change.
- `guard/`: the scroll guard. `ScrollGuardService` is an accessibility service told only about window changes (`res/xml/scroll_guard.xml`, no window content): it tracks the app in front (keyboards and the system UI pass through), and when a guarded app (`GuardStore.packages`, Instagram suggested) comes up while a habit is open and today's `ScrollBudget` is used up, it starts `GuardActivity` (accessibility services may start activities from the background). Time counts only while a guarded app is in front and the screen is on; the first grant is 10 minutes, then 5 (`ScrollGuard`, pure and tested, with the copy). Back on the guard goes to the launcher. Settings: "SCROLL GUARD ON/OFF" in Home's footer; the service can only be turned on by hand in Accessibility, and a force stop turns it off (don't `am force-stop` the app once it's on). Only activity windows count as a change of app (`getActivityInfo` resolves), so dialogs, share sheets and keyboards don't stop the count. After an update the service rebinds a few seconds later, and an app already in front is only guarded from the next switch. `GuardTracker` (domain, tested) holds the state machine.
- `widget/`: the "Today" Glance widget, `SizeMode.Exact`; colors are day/night pairs that the launcher resolves by its own theme (a launcher set to dark shows it dark); a tap on a habit opens it, a tap anywhere else opens Home (`EXTRA_OPEN_HOME`); roomy rows (name, topic, week marks, streak) when the widget is tall enough, one line per habit otherwise (on Niagara it is only about 70dp tall). It observes the shared status flow inside its session (a Glance session lives ~45 s and `update()` only recomposes it), and `HabitApp` calls `updateAll` on every data change to start fresh sessions. Colors come from `theme/Color.kt` as day/night pairs.

## Build and device

- `scripts/install.sh [debug|release]` builds, installs in place with `adb install -r`, compiles a release build ahead of time (`cmd package compile -m speed`), and launches the app. `app/src/main/baseline-prof.txt` is hand-written (generating one would uninstall the app on the phone). It refuses to run without the personal key (`aht.*` properties in `~/.gradle/gradle.properties`).
- Target device: Pixel 8 Pro, Android 17. `minSdk` 34.
- Bump `versionCode` for every build installed on the phone that changes the database, and ship a Room migration for it (`AutoMigration` when Room can infer it; the exported schema in `app/schemas/` must be committed). There are no migration tests yet: `MigrationTestHelper` is instrumented and needs an emulator, see below.
- **Never run `connectedAndroidTest` / `connectedCheck` on a phone in use**: AGP uninstalls the app after instrumented tests, wiping its data. Unit tests (`testDebugUnitTest`) run on the JVM; keep logic in `domain/` so it stays testable there. Use an emulator if instrumented tests are ever needed.
- Inspect scheduled reminders with `adb shell dumpsys alarm` and posted ones with `adb shell dumpsys notification --noredact`.
- **The phone runs the release build** (`scripts/install.sh release`: R8 + resource shrinking, non-debuggable; Compose in debug is visibly janky). `run-as` only works on the debug build (same key, so switching variants keeps data).
- R8 runs in full mode and strips constructors only reached by reflection. It once broke the widget (WorkManager's `OverwritingInputMerger`, which Glance sessions need: the widget stayed on its loading spinner). Keep rules live in `app/proguard-rules.pro`; WorkManager is pinned to a current version (Glance alone pulls 2.7). A Glance `ActionCallback` would need a keep rule for its no-arg constructor too. After a release install, check `adb logcat` for `WM-` errors and that `adb shell dumpsys appwidget` shows `views=` for the widget.
- Measure smoothness with `adb shell dumpsys gfxinfo dev.eduarddragu.anotherhabittracker` (reset first), only when the phone is awake and the app is in the foreground.
- README screenshots live in `docs/assets/screenshots/` (540x1111, light theme only). To redo them: set `animator_duration_scale` to 0 (entrances land at once; delete the setting afterwards), Android demo mode for the status bar, `cmd uimode night no` (not `auto`: at night auto is dark) and back to `auto`, crop 110px of status bar.
- Notification sounds are synthesized by `scripts/make_chimes.py` into `res/raw/`.

## Curriculum

- `app/src/main/assets/curriculum.json`: areas (name, weight) and topics (`id`, `area`, `title`, `requires`, `hints`), bundled into the APK. A curriculum change ships with a rebuild; user data is untouched.
- `id` is `<area>-<slug>`. **Never rename or delete an id that sessions reference**; to retire a topic, remove it from its dependents' `requires`.
- `requires` lists direct prerequisites only (nothing already implied by another entry). Topics fit in about 30 minutes; if one keeps scoring 2 or lower, split it. `hints` are 3 or 4 guiding questions, never answers.
- `TopicPickerTest.bundledCurriculumIsValidAndListsDirectPrerequisitesOnly` validates the file: run the unit tests after every edit.
