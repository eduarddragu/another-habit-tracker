# CLAUDE.md

Personal Android habit tracker (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3). The owner's private notes (background, decisions, device quirks, the weekly review) live outside the repo in `~/Repositories/.claude/context/another-habit-tracker.md`: read them first when they exist.

## Rules

- **This repository is public.** Never commit signing material, passwords, personal data, exports, logs of sessions or review notes. Anything personal goes in the private notes above, never here.
- Never change `applicationId` (`dev.eduarddragu.anotherhabittracker`) or the signing setup: either one breaks in-place updates and loses data on the phone.
- Don't commit or push unless explicitly asked.
- Code, comments, docs and UI strings in English. Plain prose, no em dashes.
- Use the `android` CLI (with `--no-metrics`) and `android docs search` for current API guidance instead of relying on memory. Verify on the device with `android screen capture` / `android layout` when a change is visible.

## Layout

- Visual identity is shared with the owner's site (eduarddragu.dev): same accent (#B04619 light, #E8743F dark), type roles, mono capitals for labels, radii (12 controls, 24 cards) and motion tokens (`Motion.EaseUi`/`EaseEntrance`/`EasePress`, 70 ms stagger, 520 ms entrance). The spec lives in the private notes (`design-system.md` next to the file above); change both together.

- `domain/`: pure Kotlin, unit tested: streaks, freezes, reminder timing, tone and copy, the home's greeting (rotating per time of day, stable within a day) and line of the day, `Practices` (meditation's daily practice, a fixed shuffled cycle of original three-step practices), heatmap cells, stats, curriculum and topic picker, `HabitSummaries.build` (everything a screen, widget or reminder needs about a habit on a day), `TodaysTopics.resolve` (the study pick's precedence: a focus for today, then a topic held through time off, then the picker), `Restore.entriesToKeep`, and `Books` (reading habits, `Books.appliesTo`: SIMPLE kind with the BOOKMARK icon or "read" in the name; the book is `Entry.track`, the author `Entry.module`, and an `EntryType.FINISHED` mark on a day closes it; `Books.shelf` gives the current book and the finished list, `Books.day` the reading card: title, author and one line per situation, seeded by day, using the habit's session minutes). FINISHED, like KNOWN, is a mark: never a session, a day done, minutes, a freeze or a heatmap cell. `PhoneClock` follows the phone's current timezone.
- `data/`: Room entities (`Habit`, `Entry`), DAOs, `HabitRepository` (the current day comes from a minute poll plus `refreshDay()`, which the midnight and clock receivers and `MainActivity.onResume` call, since the poll stalls in deep sleep). "Keep going" (a deep dive): `Continuation.candidate` offers the last session's topic (yesterday or the day before, nothing logged today) on that session's row in Recent; `FocusStore` holds the choice for today and the repository swaps it in as the pick (`PickKind.CONTINUE`), so screens, widget and reminders agree; "Back to today's topic" on the topic card undoes it. `PickHistory` keeps the study topic each day last showed (14 days, SharedPreferences); a session logged for yesterday takes its topic from there, falling back to `TopicPicker.pickOn` (the pick as it was on that day). Statuses are computed once per change on `Dispatchers.Default` and shared (`shareIn`) by every screen, the widget and `HabitApp`. Habits: Study, Meditation and Reading (paper books, 20 minutes, added by the schema 5 migration `AddReading`, which also moved Study and Meditation still on their first times off working hours on weekdays); `Habit.sessionMinutes` (schema 5, null = the kind's default, `SessionTimer.defaultMinutes`/`presets`: 20 gives 10/20/30) sets the one-tap session and the log presets, chosen in settings ("Session length"). Schemas are exported to `app/schemas/` and committed.
- `reminders/`: notification actions go through `NotificationActionReceiver` ("Done" for habits without a score logs with the last session's minutes; "On it" sets `QuietStore` for two hours, and `ReminderPlan.quieted` skips every tone but the last call); at most three actions show, so study gets Log and On it, simple habits Done, their app and On it. `ReminderScheduler` (one exact alarm per habit and slot, at most 24 slots, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`), `ReminderReceiver` (reschedules its slot, drops the reminder when its alarm, `EXTRA_TRIGGER_AT`, is from another day or over an hour late (`ReminderPlan.tooLate`), then notifies only if the day is still open), `RescheduleReceiver` (boot, update, time and timezone changes; only after a reboot it posts the latest missed slot once: the phone corrects its clock every day or two, and posting then brought back reminders already swiped away), `MidnightReceiver` (exact alarm at 00:00:30: clears yesterday's reminders, moves the day on, renews every reminder chain, rolls widgets over, asks for a backup), `Notifications` (channels `reminders_v4` and `last_call_v4`, with synthesized chimes addressed by name, `raw/<name>`, kept from the shrinker by `res/raw/keep.xml`, and vibration; channel sound/vibration can't be changed after creation, so behaviour changes need new ids). Receiver work runs in `HabitApp.appScope`, whose exception handler logs instead of crashing.
- Log form: `LogEntry(habitId, entryId)`; with an `entryId` it edits or deletes that session ("Edit session", Save changes, Delete session with a confirmation). The form has no day switch: new logs are for today, and yesterday (or a frozen day) is logged from the habit's page ("Missed yesterday. Log it" whenever yesterday is empty, `Freezes.yesterdayEmpty`, with "Freeze it" when `canSaveYesterday`; or a freeze in Recent), which opens `LogEntry(onDay = …)`; "Freeze today instead" freezes the day the form opened on; a session on a frozen day replaces the freeze (and a freeze sharing a day with a session is ignored in summaries and dropped on restore). The score line comes from `TopicPicker.scoreMeaning`. The habit page shows no line under the week (only a milestone, on its day); its stat row reads "1h 45m" over "TIME · 30 DAYS" (`formatDuration`, units on the number, the window in the caption); the topic card's curriculum link sits top right on the label row ("N due" when reviews wait). Recent lists sessions and freezes (a freeze is a hollow dot; tapped, it offers "Log a session" (which replaces it), "Remove freeze" or Cancel); a missed yesterday can be frozen from the habit page (`Freezes.canSaveYesterday`); a day off yesterday with no session reads "Did it yesterday? Log it" (`HabitSummary.yesterdayOff`), never with a freeze. "I know this" and "Mark the whole area as known" come with an Undo snackbar, and so do freezing (yesterday or today), removing a freeze, discarding a finished session and removing time off (the repository's app-scope restore methods and `Sessions.restore`). Habit settings with unsaved changes, and the log form with an unsaved note, ask before going back (`BackHandler`).
- `ui/`: Compose screens (home, habit detail with today's topic, log, curriculum, habit settings, backup, scroll guard settings, time off) on Navigation 3. Screens are edge to edge: they fill the window and use `screenPadding()` as scroll content padding. Per-habit ViewModels extend `HabitViewModel` (live status, `once {}` guard against double taps). Habit icons are line vectors in `res/drawable/ic_habit_*.xml` (24dp, 1.75 stroke), chosen per habit (`Habit.icon`, a `HabitIcon` name; null means the kind's default). Home is three zones down the screen: date, greeting (the name in the accent italic, revealed by a soft left-to-right edge while it sharpens from a blur) and line; the `Planet` mark (butter disc, tilted ring; endless idle loop while visible: the ring swings, opens and closes, the disc breathes) in the middle; one card per habit at the bottom (from three habits on the cards are compact, a one-line title and the line under it only at the last call, with less air around the planet, so Home fits one screen; the page scrolls only when it doesn't fit, with no overscroll otherwise), and one footer line, "BACKUP · SCROLL GUARD · TIME OFF", each a label with its state in the accent. Reading's page and Home card show the current book (`TodaysBook` in a `DayCard` where meditation shows its practice), a BOOKS section lists the current book ("Mark as finished", with Undo) and the finished ones, and its log form has Book (prefilled), Author and "Finished it" (`HabitRepository.saveReading`, one transaction with the day's FINISHED mark). The full entrance plays at app start; coming back from outside the app replays the planet and the cards (reset on ON_STOP so they never blink off on screen), coming back from a habit's page brings the cards up again, lighter, with the slide. The streak and `WeekStrip` (this week, Monday to Sunday) are on every home card, the detail page and the widget; the heatmap only appears after eight weeks of history (`Heatmap.worthShowing`) and starts at the first log. Logging plays a commit on the screen underneath: the log form posts a `Commit` after the write, and `rememberCommit` fills the day, rolls the streak (`RollingNumber`) and swaps the labels once the sheet is gone (timings in `Motion.Commit`), with the Confirm haptic; a same-day log shows no snackbar. Minutes are presets (study 30/45/60, other habits 5/10/15, or built around the habit's `sessionMinutes`), plus a session's real length as its own chip when the log comes from a finished focus session. Shared building blocks in `ui/components/Common.kt`: `SectionLabel` (every section heading: mono capitals in the accent), `DayCard` (today's topic or practice: plain while open, warm once done, explicit onSurface content color), `NumberedSteps`, `LogButton` ("Log" / "Log again"), `cardOutline()` (outlined buttons on tonal cards), `Chevron`, `CardLabel` (a card's muted label with only the state in the accent, shared by Home, the habit page and the session card), `TextAction` (accent text with no button box, for every text action at a row's edge or on its own line: a TextButton centres short labels in a 58dp minimum width, so they never sit flush), `touchTarget` (a 48dp touch area that doesn't change the layout size, so lines don't grow; `TextAction` uses it), `rememberTouchExploration`. Accessibility: `domain/Descriptions` builds what TalkBack reads for the week strip, heatmap and streak (`HabitSummary.daysOff` tells days off from freezes); custom controls carry roles, state descriptions and selectable groups. Rules: every card uses `shapes.large` (24dp), controls `shapes.medium` (12dp, passed explicitly: Material buttons default to a pill); sections 24dp apart, 8dp inside, 12dp inside cards; card labels are muted with only the state in the accent; text buttons trailing a row are flush right (zero horizontal padding), leading ones flush left; numbers use `NumeralsDisplay` / `NumeralsLarge` / `Numerals` / `NumeralsSmall`; swaps use `fadeThrough()`. Mixed faces or sizes on one line align by baseline (`alignByBaseline`, `alignBy(LastBaseline)`), never by box; big numbers use `opticalStart` so the first digit's empty side (DM Sans' "1" especially) doesn't look like an indent; the Recent timeline dot is placed from the date's measured baseline minus half the mono cap height. `theme/` holds the palette (the accent is a touch deeper in light, #B04619, and lighter in dark, #E8743F, than the site's, so small accent text reaches 4.5:1 on the tonal cards), the bundled fonts (Cormorant Garamond for titles, always with lining figures (`lnum`), since its default old-style digits read as letters; DM Sans for text and for values via `Numerals` (DM Sans has no tabular figures), Geist Mono for small labels; all four font files are subset to Latin, punctuation and arrows with fonttools (`pyftsubset --unicodes=U+0000-00FF,U+0131,U+0152-0153,U+02BB-02BC,U+02C6,U+02DA,U+02DC,U+2000-206F,U+2074,U+20AC,U+2122,U+2190-2193,U+2212,U+2215,U+FEFF,U+FFFD --layout-features='*' --no-hinting`), so a character outside that set in the curriculum or copy needs a new subset; OFL licenses in `assets/licenses/`) and `Motion` (easing and durations from the site: slide transitions, never fade two screens at once; the push is 520 ms so it matches the slow home, and a pushed page's content arrives in steps via `rememberArrival`, once per page).
- `backup/`: `BackupStore` and `BackupWorker`. One versioned JSON document (`domain/Backup.kt`, DTOs separate from Room, strict validation, a golden v1 file in the tests). Manual export and import through SAF; `Backups.decode` rejects dates before 2000 or after tomorrow (time off up to two years ahead), sessions over 1440 minutes, habit id 0 and duplicate or overlapping time off, and files over 20 MB are refused before reading; import replaces everything in one transaction after saving the current data to `before-import-<time>.json` (the newest 5 are kept), then clears the per-device stores keyed by habit id (session, focus, `PickHistory`, `QuietStore`). A nightly file chosen once (persisted grant, e.g. on Drive) is rewritten by a WorkManager job two minutes after every change and after midnight, then read back to verify (one save at a time, a process-wide mutex; an immediate save, after midnight or a restore, runs as `backup_now` so the debounce can't push it back); a local copy goes to the app's external files folder for `adb pull`. Stopping the nightly file asks first. Status shows at the bottom of Home, as a warning when it failed or is 48 h old. Bump `Backups.VERSION` for any field change.
- Time off (`domain/TimeOff`, Room table `time_off`, schema 4): periods with a start that may be up to 60 days back and an optional end. Days off bridge streaks like freezes without using the week's freeze, can't be frozen themselves (`HabitRepository.freeze` refuses), show as dashed cells, and make `HabitSummary.pausedToday` true, so `dayOpen` is false: no reminders, no last call, no guard, no recap; a session logged on one still counts. Screen `ui/timeoff/TimeOffScreen.kt` ("TIME OFF" in Home's footer; Home's line and the cards' context line become a gentle `Motivation.timeOffLine`, rotating by day, instead of reminder times). The study topic of the first day off is held through it and offered again on return (`TimeOff.heldSince`, as CONTINUING) until a study session is logged. Starting one clears reminders already up.
- Reminder times per day type: `Habit.weekendReminderTimes` (schema 4, null = same as weekdays) and `Habit.reminderTimesOn(day)`; slot n is the n-th time of whichever day comes next (`ReminderPlan.nextSlotTrigger`), so weekdays and weekends can have different lists. Settings show "Reminders · weekdays" and "· weekend" ("Different times on weekends" / "Same as weekdays"). Backup format version 3 carries both (`timeOff`, `weekendReminderTimes`), version 4 adds `sessionMinutes` and FINISHED marks; older files still import.
- Weekly recap: `RecapReceiver` on an exact alarm every Sunday at 20:30 (`WeeklyRecap.nextAt`, renewed with the midnight chain), text from `WeeklyRecap.build` (days per habit, minutes, study average, the worst-scored topic, the longest streak), channel `recap_v1`. Channels are created again when `CHANNELS_VERSION` changes. Milestones (`Milestones`: 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365) replace the line of the day on Home and the streak line on the habit page on the day they're reached. Due reviews (`TopicPicker.dueReviews`, weakest first) are listed at the top of the curriculum with "Study today" (sets the focus, which reads as REVIEW when due), and counted on the topic card's curriculum link.
- Focus session ("the planet is the timer"): one tap starts 30 minutes for study, 5 for meditation (`SessionTimer.defaultMinutes`), from the planet on Home ("Start 30 min" under it) or the topic/practice card, which then goes back to Home (the session card there keeps the topic and its questions, and a tap on it or on the countdown notification opens the habit's page). `FocusSession` (domain, tested) is on the elapsed-realtime clock; `SessionStore` persists it and shifts it after a reboot (`SessionTimer.rebootShift`, tested); `reminders/Sessions` is the only way to change it (synchronized): store, then the ongoing notification (topic, questions, countdown chronometer, promoted to a status bar chip that shows the chronometer while running and "Paused" while paused, channel `session_v1`; swiped away, it sets `SessionStore.hidden` and `Sessions.refresh` doesn't repost it after a reboot or update until the session changes by hand) and an exact ELAPSED_REALTIME alarm to `SessionReceiver`, which posts the end (channel `session_end_v1`, chime) with Log (prefilled minutes via `EXTRA_LOG_MINUTES`/`EXTRA_LOG_DAY`) and +5 min. On Home the Hero and cards cross-fade to `SessionHeader`/`SessionCard`; `Planet` morphs (disc 88 to 168dp, ring opens into a track, accent arc drains, closes in the accent when done) with the idle loop off; the screen stays on while it runs. The session tick is never read in composition (only in draw or through `derivedStateOf`) and runs only while the session is running. Saving a log clears only the finished session of that day; reminders stay quiet for the habit while it runs; midnight ends stale sessions silently (`Sessions.endQuietly`); a restore clears it.
- `guard/`: the scroll guard. `ScrollGuardService` is an accessibility service told only about window changes and scrolls (`res/xml/scroll_guard.xml`, no window content): it tracks the app in front (keyboards and the system UI pass through), and when a guarded app (`GuardStore.packages`, Instagram suggested) comes up while a habit is open and today's `ScrollBudget` is used up, it starts `GuardActivity` (accessibility services may start activities from the background). Time counts only while a guarded app is in front and the screen is on, and time across midnight is split between the two days (`GuardAction.Use(day, millis)`); the first grant is 10 minutes, then 5 (`ScrollGuard`, pure and tested, with the copy). Back on the guard goes to the launcher. Settings: "SCROLL GUARD ON/OFF" in Home's footer, with this week's count of blocks and minutes let through (`GuardStore.days`, 14 days kept, `GuardWeek`); the service can only be turned on by hand in Accessibility, and a force stop turns it off (don't `am force-stop` the app once it's on). Only activity windows count as a change of app (`getActivityInfo` resolves), so dialogs, share sheets and keyboards don't stop the count. A scroll from a guarded app also counts as it being in front (it can come back through Recents without a window change), except within 500 ms after a window change, when it's a leftover of a fling. Evaluations use `repository.statuses()` (computed for the clock's today), never the shared flow, which lags around midnight. `GuardTracker` (domain, tested) holds the state machine.
- `widget/`: the "Today" Glance widget, `SizeMode.Exact`; colors are day/night pairs that the launcher resolves by its own theme (a launcher set to dark shows it dark); habits off today are left out of "N OF M" ("DAY OFF" when all are); a tap on a habit opens it, a tap anywhere else opens Home (`EXTRA_OPEN_HOME`); roomy rows (name, topic, week marks, streak) when the widget is tall enough, one line per habit otherwise (on Niagara it is only about 70dp tall). It observes the shared status flow inside its session (a Glance session lives ~45 s and `update()` only recomposes it), and `HabitApp` calls `updateAll` on every data change to start fresh sessions. Colors come from `theme/Color.kt` as day/night pairs.

## Build and device

- `scripts/install.sh [debug|release]` builds, installs in place with `adb install -r`, compiles a release build ahead of time (`cmd package compile -m speed`), and launches the app. `app/src/main/baseline-prof.txt` is hand-written (generating one would uninstall the app on the phone). It refuses to run without the personal key (`aht.*` properties in `~/.gradle/gradle.properties`).
- Target device: Pixel 8 Pro, Android 17. `minSdk` 34.
- Bump `versionCode` for every build installed on the phone that changes the database, and ship a Room migration for it (`AutoMigration` when Room can infer it; the exported schema in `app/schemas/` must be committed). There are no migration tests yet: `MigrationTestHelper` is instrumented and needs an emulator, see below.
- **Never run `connectedAndroidTest` / `connectedCheck` on a phone in use**: AGP uninstalls the app after instrumented tests, wiping its data. Unit tests (`testDebugUnitTest`) run on the JVM; keep logic in `domain/` so it stays testable there. Use an emulator if instrumented tests are ever needed.
- Inspect scheduled reminders with `adb shell dumpsys alarm` and posted ones with `adb shell dumpsys notification --noredact`.
- **The phone runs the release build** (`scripts/install.sh release`: R8 + resource shrinking, non-debuggable; Compose in debug is visibly janky). `run-as` only works on the debug build (same key, so switching variants keeps data).
- R8 runs in full mode and strips constructors only reached by reflection. It once broke the widget (WorkManager's `OverwritingInputMerger`, which Glance sessions need: the widget stayed on its loading spinner). Keep rules live in `app/proguard-rules.pro`; WorkManager is pinned to a current version (Glance alone pulls 2.7). A Glance `ActionCallback` would need a keep rule for its no-arg constructor too. After a release install, check `adb logcat` for `WM-` errors and that `adb shell dumpsys appwidget` shows `views=` for the widget.
- Measure smoothness with `adb shell dumpsys gfxinfo dev.eduarddragu.anotherhabittracker` (reset first), only when the phone is awake and the app is in the foreground.
- README screenshots live in `docs/assets/screenshots/` (540x1111): home, study, log, curriculum, guard, each as `<name>.png` and `<name>-dark.png`, the README shows the light ones only (a `<picture>` follows the browser's scheme, not GitHub's theme, so a dark Mac saw dark screens on light GitHub); the dark ones are used by the site's carousel. To redo them: set `animator_duration_scale` to 0 (entrances land at once; delete the setting afterwards), Android demo mode for the status bar, capture each screen with `cmd uimode night no` then `yes` (not `auto`: at night auto is dark) and back to `auto`, crop 110px of status bar and 60px of navigation bar. The guard comes from its settings' Preview, which saves nothing.
- Notification sounds are synthesized by `scripts/make_chimes.py` into `res/raw/`.

## Curriculum

- `app/src/main/assets/curriculum.json`: areas (name, weight) and topics (`id`, `area`, `title`, `requires`, `hints`), bundled into the APK. A curriculum change ships with a rebuild; user data is untouched.
- `id` is `<area>-<slug>`. **Never rename or delete an id that sessions reference**; to retire a topic, remove it from its dependents' `requires`.
- `requires` lists direct prerequisites only (nothing already implied by another entry). Topics fit in about 30 minutes; if one keeps scoring 2 or lower, split it. `hints` are 3 or 4 guiding questions, never answers.
- `TopicPickerTest.bundledCurriculumIsValidAndListsDirectPrerequisitesOnly` validates the file: run the unit tests after every edit.
